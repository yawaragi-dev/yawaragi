Nocturne, inverted

Nocturne's structure kept whole — compact 0.7× spacing, 8px radii, outlined primary actions, rules that fade at their ends, elevation as a hairline plus ambient shade. Two things change: the ground warms from blue-grey to ash so bottle photography stays honest in dim rooms, and the accent moves from blurple to ginshu (銀朱, vermilion knocked back with ash) — the seal on a sake label, related to the object without matching its gold and kraft. Every value below is a CSS custom property, so a later native port reads one token table rather than a stylesheet.

Neutral ramp

Warm-neutral rather than Nocturne's blue-grey, so labels are not cooled by the UI around them. Steps count outward from the ground; 500 is the lowest step legible as 11px metadata, body-size muted text uses 600 or above. The camera and overlay ground is a fixed #121110, below the ramp.

Core components

Actions
Primary stays an outline, never a fill — Nocturne's rule, and it keeps ginshu a mark rather than a flood.
Rating
Five stars, half steps, 34px, each split into two tap halves. Tapping saves — there is no Save button; an undo notice covers mistakes. Filled stars are accent-600, empty neutral-400. Read-only rows use the same glyph at 11–13px.
Tags

Placeholders
bottle photography
Every image is a striped slot with a monospace label until real photography lands. On this dark ground Nocturne’s .lighten blend applies again — photographs of bottles on black fall into the page. Slots use neutral stripes, never light ones.
