Three things, done well

Identify
Kanji or romaji — the camera reads whichever the bottle gives you. No camera? Use a photo, or type the name.
Rate in one tap
The star is the save. Add a note if you want, or detailed tasting notes later.
Learn your palate
After three tastings it shows what you like — and why. Already know whisky or wine? Start from a drink you love.
Your journal stays yours
Everything works on your phone without an account. Nothing is posted publicly, and your ratings are only shared — anonymously — if you choose to.
